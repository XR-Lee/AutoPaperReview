Title: A Synthetic Tracking Study

Abstract
The proposed system achieves 95% accuracy and generalizes to unseen sequences.

Methods
Eight temporally related images from one specimen were randomly divided at image level. Six images were used for training and two for testing.

Results
Accuracy was reported on the two held-out images. No specimen-level split, confidence interval, repeated seed, or external test set was reported.

Conclusion
The method is ready for deployment across unseen structures.
