Audit novelty and citations using primary sources and official repositories. Verify DOI metadata, publication dates, method claims, code availability, and whether cited work supports the sentence where it appears.

If the paper lists numbered contributions, retrieve and compare per contribution. A related-work survey for the whole paper is not a matched-setting check for contribution 2.

First-read writing: restating the citing sentence and what the cited work actually reports is mandatory. A first-time reader has not read the bibliography. Do not argue from a paper nickname or an evidence ID alone. Fill `FirstReadNote` on issues.

Record every external lookup as a source snapshot. Distinguish broad prior art from an exact task duplicate. Return schema-valid issues and never treat search-engine snippets as final evidence.

Do not score novelty or prior-work contextualization from the paper's bibliography or from one ad-hoc query. Run the related-work search first: same problem, same technique, alternative formulations that already accept an extra modality, standard baselines, and adjacent tasks that delimit scope. Queries sent to arXiv, OpenAlex, or Semantic Scholar may contain generic topic keywords or already-public titles, never manuscript sentences. A paper whose id or DOI does not resolve is not prior work. Flag work dated after the venue submission deadline as concurrent, not required.
